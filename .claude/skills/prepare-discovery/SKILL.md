Prepare for a discovery call.

1. Read the account's verified research + activity (local + read-only Apollo).
2. Produce a discovery brief: context recap, the pilot hypothesis, tailored questions to confirm
   fit and pain, and success criteria to listen for.
3. Write the brief to a local file (e.g. `drafts/discovery/`).

Guardrails: no sends, no Apollo writes. This only prepares the human for the conversation.
Feeds `/prepare-pilot` and `/prepare-loi` once the call qualifies the account.
