Draft an LOI.

1. Confirm discovery notes + a pilot proposal exist. If not, stop.
2. Delegate to the **loi-preparer** subagent.
3. Produce an LOI draft with placeholders for names, dates, scope, and signatures for a human to
   review and finalize. Keep terms consistent with the pilot proposal.

Guardrails: no email-send, no Apollo, no web. The LOI is a draft; a human negotiates and signs.
