Draft a pilot proposal.

1. Confirm the account is qualified (discovery notes exist). If not, stop.
2. Delegate to the **loi-preparer** subagent, passing the discovery notes.
3. Produce a short free, time-boxed pilot proposal: scope, timeline, success criteria, and the
   path to a signed LOI. Ground every commitment in the discovery notes — promise nothing extra.

Guardrails: no email-send, no Apollo, no web. Output is a local draft for a human to finalize.
